# Local Quote Follow-Up

## When to Use This Skill

- The user sent an estimate and never heard back.
- The business has quote-sent leads stuck in the pipeline.
- The user wants follow-ups that are persistent but not annoying.

## When Not to Use This Skill

- The person is only a fresh lead and has not received a quote.
- The task is a review request after completed work.

## Intake Questions

Ask only for missing information that changes the output. If enough context exists, proceed and state assumptions.

- Service, quote amount or range, sent date, prospect concern, and close deadline.
- Was the quote Good/Better/Best or single-option?
- What proof can be used: before/after, testimonial, warranty, availability, or financing?

## Operating Workflow

- Send day-0 confirmation that the estimate was delivered and invite questions.
- Send day-3 clarification touch with a binary call option.
- Send day-7 value-add touch with proof, before/after, seasonal urgency, or adjusted scope.
- Send day-14 breakup that leaves the door open.
- Avoid guilt, pressure, or generic just checking in.
- Record outcome as won, lost, delayed, ghosted, or needs revised scope.

## Output Contract

Always produce practical artifacts the user can copy, paste, configure, or hand to a contractor.

- SMS and email versions for each touch.
- Subject lines.
- Value-add prompt bank.
- Pipeline automation rules.
- Lost reason tracking fields.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- Every touch contains new value or clarity.
- The ask is easy to answer.
- The sequence stops when the customer replies or books.

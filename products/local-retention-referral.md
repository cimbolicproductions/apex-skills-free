# Local Retention and Referral System

## When to Use This Skill

- The business finishes jobs and never follows up.
- The user wants repeat customers, referrals, seasonal reminders, or customer lifetime value improvement.
- The service has maintenance, replacement, rental, warranty, or annual checkup potential.

## When Not to Use This Skill

- The customer is not yet booked.
- The task is public review generation only.

## Intake Questions

Ask only for missing information that changes the output. If enough context exists, proceed and state assumptions.

- Service type, repeat interval, referral incentive, customer segments, and seasonal triggers.
- What customer action should happen after the job?
- What CRM or email/SMS tool is available?

## Operating Workflow

- Create post-job sequence: thank you, review, check-in, referral ask, seasonal reminder.
- Segment customers by service type, value, satisfaction, and repeat timing.
- Use referral offers that are simple, trackable, and profitable.
- Add seasonal reactivation messages before demand peaks.
- Calculate estimated lifetime value to justify retention spend.

## Output Contract

Always produce practical artifacts the user can copy, paste, configure, or hand to a contractor.

- Day 0, day 3, day 30, day 90, and seasonal message templates.
- Referral offer options.
- Reactivation calendar.
- LTV calculation prompts.
- CRM automation rules.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- Messages feel like service, not spam.
- Referral reward economics are clear.
- Timing matches the service cycle.

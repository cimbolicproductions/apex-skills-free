# Local Review Generation System

## When to Use This Skill

- A local business needs more Google reviews.
- The user wants post-job review automation.
- The business needs templates for positive, neutral, or negative review responses.

## When Not to Use This Skill

- The user wants to manipulate or buy fake reviews.
- The issue is a public crisis or legal dispute requiring legal review.

## Intake Questions

Ask only for missing information that changes the output. If enough context exists, proceed and state assumptions.

- Where should reviews go: Google, Facebook, Yelp, industry platform, or all?
- What CRM or job-complete trigger exists?
- What is the review link and customer communication channel?

## Operating Workflow

- Trigger from job completion, not from memory.
- Ask first as feedback, then route happy customers to the public review link.
- Route unhappy feedback to owner resolution privately without suppressing legitimate criticism dishonestly.
- Send SMS day 0, email day 3, and final reminder day 10.
- Respond to every review within 24 to 48 hours.
- Use customer-specific details without revealing private information.

## Output Contract

Always produce practical artifacts the user can copy, paste, configure, or hand to a contractor.

- Review request SMS and email templates.
- Positive/negative routing logic.
- Google review link instructions.
- Review response templates.
- Automation setup checklist.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- No incentives are offered in ways that violate platform policy.
- Negative-review responses are calm, brief, and take resolution offline.
- The workflow is triggered automatically after completion.

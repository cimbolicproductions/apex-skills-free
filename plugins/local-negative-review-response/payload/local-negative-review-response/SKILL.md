# Negative Review Response Handler

## When to Use This Skill

- The user needs to respond to a bad review or public complaint.
- A review may be fake, unfair, exaggerated, or policy-violating.
- The business wants to protect reputation without sounding defensive.

## When Not to Use This Skill

- The issue involves legal threats, discrimination, injury, or regulated claims; recommend legal review.
- The user wants to attack the reviewer or reveal private customer information.

## Intake Questions

Ask only for missing information that changes the output. If enough context exists, proceed and state assumptions.

- Review text, star rating, platform, what actually happened, and desired resolution.
- Is the reviewer a real customer?
- What private details must not be disclosed?

## Operating Workflow

- Acknowledge concern without admitting facts not yet verified.
- Stay under 150 words for public replies.
- Move resolution offline with a direct contact path.
- Do not argue, accuse, reveal private info, or mention internal notes.
- If fake or policy-violating, prepare a flagging checklist separately from the public response.
- After resolution, politely ask if the customer would consider updating their review.

## Output Contract

Always produce practical artifacts the user can copy, paste, configure, or hand to a contractor.

- Public response draft.
- Private resolution message.
- Internal escalation checklist.
- Review update request.
- Fake-review flagging guidance.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- The response reassures future customers, not just the reviewer.
- No private facts are exposed.
- Tone is calm, accountable, and brief.

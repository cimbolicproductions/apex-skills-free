# Execution Standard

Read the entire skill and any required packaged companion before acting. Fill templates as written. Use `unknown` for unavailable values and name the required input.

Never invent facts, quotes, URLs, prices, services, access, completed actions or measured outcomes. Distinguish direct observations from hypotheses and owner recollection. Record actual source URLs and verification dates.

Verify by doing: inspect the real rendered page, export or signed-out listing. Keep a record of the before state and verify the after state. A blocked required check means the run is incomplete; say which check and what the owner must supply. Use n/a only when a check genuinely does not apply.

Stay within the user's authorized scope. Audits and drafts do not authorize publication or changes to live business listings. Get authorization for those actions, and leave sign-in and ownership verification to the owner. Do not send messages without explicit authorization.

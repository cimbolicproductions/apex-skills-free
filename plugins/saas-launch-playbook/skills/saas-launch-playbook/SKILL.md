# SaaS Launch Playbook

## When to Use This Skill

- The user wants to launch a SaaS, app, plugin, template, or AI tool.
- The user needs launch channel selection, timeline, copy, or launch assets.
- The user is deciding between Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, Indie Hackers, LinkedIn, email, or niche communities.

## When Not to Use This Skill

- The idea has no validation and launch would only test vague curiosity.
- The user wants app store optimization specifically; use saas-aso.

## Intake Questions

Ask only for missing information that changes the output. If enough context exists, proceed and state assumptions.

- What is being launched and who is it for?
- What proof or user results exist?
- What channel already has trust or audience access?

## Operating Workflow

- Choose one primary launch channel based on audience fit, not ego.
- Validate privately with design partners before broad launch when possible.
- Prepare assets: headline, tagline, screenshots, demo GIF/video, founder story, FAQ, offer, and follow-up sequence.
- Use a 7-day pre-launch plan: warm audience, schedule posts, line up testimonials, finalize onboarding, test analytics.
- Launch with one concrete CTA and track visitor source to signup, activation, and payment.
- Run a post-launch review within 48 hours and decide what to iterate.

## Output Contract

Always produce practical artifacts the user can copy, paste, configure, or hand to a contractor.

- Launch channel matrix.
- 7-day launch timeline.
- Launch post copy.
- Founder comments/reply bank.
- Post-launch retro template.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- The launch promise is specific and memorable.
- The CTA matches the stage: buy, book, join beta, or try.
- Metrics capture source to activation, not only traffic.

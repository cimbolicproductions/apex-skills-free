# Platform Specs (Current as of 2026)

## LinkedIn
- **Post character limit**: 3,000 chars (but high-performing posts are 180-250 words)
- **Optimal length**: ~1,300 chars
- **Line break trick**: every 1-2 sentences for mobile readability
- **Hashtags**: 3 max, placed at the end
- **Links**: post performs better when link is in the FIRST COMMENT, not the body
- **Best times**: Tue-Thu 8-10am or 12-1pm in user's timezone
- **Hooks that work**: contrarian claim, specific number, named enemy, open loop
- **Hooks that fail**: questions, generic statements, "exciting news!"

## X / Twitter
- **Tweet limit**: 280 chars (free) / 25,000 chars (Premium long-form)
- **Thread limit**: no hard limit, but 5-9 tweets perform best for evergreen content
- **Hook tweet**: must stand alone — 70% of readers only see tweet 1
- **Best times**: 9-11am or 7-9pm in user's timezone
- **Image/video**: increases engagement 2-3x but not required
- **Reply CTAs underperform** "follow for more" CTAs in 2026
- **Avoid**: "1/" numbering (looks dated), emoji bullets, "🧵" thread emoji (overused)

## Newsletter (Substack / Beehiiv / ConvertKit)
- **Subject line**: 4-8 words, curiosity gap or specific outcome
- **Preheader**: 40-90 chars, expands on subject
- **Body length**: 500-800 words for high open-to-click ratio (longer for thought leadership)
- **Single CTA rule**: one ask per email, not three
- **P.S. line**: highest read rate after subject; use for soft secondary CTA
- **Personalization tokens**: first name in body increases CTR ~15%

## YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels
- **Duration**: 45-90 seconds is the sweet spot in 2026 (used to be 15-30s)
- **Hook**: first 3 seconds determine 80% of retention
- **Cadence**: write for spoken delivery, not reading
- **Captions**: hard-coded captions still increase retention ~30%
- **CTA**: verbal CTA in last 3-5 seconds; on-screen text reinforces
- **Vertical 9:16** for Shorts/Reels/TikTok; square 1:1 for IG feed

## Instagram / Threads
- **Caption limit**: 2,200 chars (IG) / 500 chars (Threads)
- **Optimal length**: 100-150 words for both
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 on IG; 0 on Threads
- **Threads format**: more conversational, less polished than IG
- **Carousels** outperform single images on IG by 1.7x

## Email Teaser
- **Subject + preheader work as a pair**
- **Body length**: 75-100 words max
- **One link, one CTA**
- **Plain-text outperforms HTML** for solo-creator newsletters
